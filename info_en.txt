Claude Usage Tray 1.0.0

A tray app that continuously shows the usage limits of your claude.ai subscription:

  •  Current session (5-hour window) and weekly usage
  •  Per-model limits, pace marker and forecast
  •  Notifications when thresholds are crossed

Authentication: it uses your Claude Code sign-in (OAuth), or you can enter the claude.ai cookie in Settings. The key is stored encrypted and bound to your Windows account only.

Unofficial software, not affiliated with Anthropic. It uses an undocumented internal endpoint that may change at any time.
