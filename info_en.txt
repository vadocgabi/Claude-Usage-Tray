Claude Usage Tray 1.0.0

A small tray app that continuously shows two usage values of your claude.ai subscription:

  •  Current session (5-hour window)
  •  Weekly usage (7-day window)

On first launch the Settings window opens: enter your organization ID (org_id) and the sessionKey cookie. The key is stored encrypted and bound to your Windows account only.

Unofficial software, not affiliated with Anthropic. It uses an undocumented internal claude.ai endpoint that may change at any time.
